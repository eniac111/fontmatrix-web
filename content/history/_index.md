![The logo of fontmatrix.be](/history/logo.png)

Pierre Marchand started Fontmatrix in October 2007, as a font manager for
[Scribus](https://www.scribus.net/) users on Linux. It grew out of Undertype,
a larger typography project hosted at Gna!, and was developed there in
Subversion with Alexandre Prokoudine, Riku Leino, Vladimir Savic, Peter Linnell,
Parag Nemade, Alessandro Rimoldi and others; Jean Ghali made the early Windows
builds. Releases 0.3, 0.4 and 0.6 followed between 2008 and
2009; 0.6.0 was the last release of that time.

After 2011 the project went quiet. The code moved to GitHub, where Alexandre
Prokoudine and a few contributors kept it building (0.9.100 in 2020), and from 2026 it was ported to Qt 6 and KDE
Frameworks 6 and released as [Fontmatrix 1.0.0](/news/fontmatrix-1.0.0/). It is
maintained by Blagovest Petrov.

The project's first home was fontmatrix.net, later fontmatrix.be. Both domains
are gone, but the [Internet Archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20151218142406/http://fontmatrix.be/)
kept the pages. They are collected here with their original text; download
links point to the archived files, and the videos, which were hosted on
blip.tv, are lost.
