Frank Waterman Fri, 21 Apr 2017 10:07:18 -0700 

Greetings to all once more,

In preparing to install Fontmatrix, of course I read all of the
documentation that was included with special attention to the installation
instructions for Debian/Ubuntu.  A couple of questions have come to my mind
that I would like to get answered (as far as possible) prior to attempting
installation.

First of all, I would like to make a comment. Examining the text files, I
noted that they are all dated 5/21/16, almost one year ago.  Given the
inexorable progress being made with almost all the component software that
comprises Linux (and other operating systems too, I'm sure.  I'm just not
as intimate with them any longer), shouldn't the documentation, especially
the installation directions be reviewed and their information updated?  I
am not being critical, I am just pointing out that this has the potential
to be a source of confusion and frustration for end users who are not as
knowledgeable (and I know Ubuntu has a few of them having perused the
official (as well as offensive) help forum).  As I did a fair amount of
technical writing in the course of my work, I will volunteer my services to
do the 'heavy lifting' portion of updating the install files provided I may
count on the assistance of some/all of you advising me.  Also, please be
advised that I am also working on a project creating a user's guide for an
Android e-book reader, so I will have to share my time equally between
Fontmatrix's needs, the e-book reader and my own, personal projects (which
I am rapidly trying to reduce to have more time to apply to other projects).

Moving along now to the questions I have.  First, I have been unable to
find any discernible reference to which version of Fontmatrix I have
downloaded.  In the ToDo documentation, I have seen references to 0.2 and
to 0.3.  In examining the desktop file in my text editor, it looks like it
might be referenced as 1.0, but all of this is just supposition on my
part.  So would someone please fill in the FNG about which version is
currently being distributed?

As to the second question:  Never mind.  I had some concerns about
Fontmatrix's dependencies, but I was able to reason out the answer for
myself.

Happy days all!
