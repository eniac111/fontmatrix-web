title: Fontmatrix

Fontmatrix keeps a large font collection in order. It activates and deactivates
fonts and families for the other applications on your system, so only the fonts
a job needs are in their menus. It runs on Linux and Windows.

## What it does

- **Activate and deactivate fonts** for the other applications, for yourself or
  for every user of the computer.
- **Tag fonts** and group them into sets.
- **Find fonts** with simple or combined filters: tags, metadata,
  [PANOSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PANOSE) classification, language
  coverage, licence, and font technology.
- **Inspect a font in detail**: glyphs, OpenType features, name and TrueType tables.
- **Try out OpenType features** in the playground, with HarfBuzz shaping and
  right-to-left scripts.
- **Variable and colour fonts**: move the axes of a variable font, and see CBDT,
  sbix, COLR and OpenType-SVG glyphs in colour.
- **Compare the glyphs** of several fonts on top of each other, useful for
  learning type design.
- **Find duplicates**: identical files, or the same font in files that differ.
- **Produce font books** as PDF, and extract the fonts embedded in a PDF file.
