[Undertype-users] fontmatrix experience

Tiggeler, Jan 42 Thu, 10 Jan 2008 21:49:46 -0800 

First of I would like to say a great "Thank You" to all involved for bringing 
us such a long awaited application - a real fontmanager for Linux for the first 
time, at least to my knowledge. Something I really desired for quite a long 
time. And for seeing it being actively developed. Defenitely great!  I have 
played around a bit in Ubuntu Gutsy and it was all in all a very pleasing 
experience. But I came across one or two niggles. The first is one that may not 
occur in every day use but since I tried to import a heavy mass of Type1-Fonts 
for use in OpenOffice (a shame that it can't use Opentype nowadays) Fontmatrix 
refused to load afterwards. In fact it was about 2000 Fonts what means in Type1 
a load of nearly 10.000 files since I was told you need in OO all the five 
corresponding files. Maybe I can't blame Fontmatrix for that - such a 
quantitymay bemore than it is made for (and to be honest - in reality I don't 
need them all...)  And I have a little wish: When importing another folder - 
could the opening filebrowser remember the last opened folder? And would it be 
possible to select and import multiple files resp. folders? That could make 
things easier.  All the best  Jan
