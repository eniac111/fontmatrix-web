Fontmatrix was discussed on *undertype-users*, the mailing list of Undertype,
the typography project at Gna! that Fontmatrix grew out of. Gna! closed in
2017 and took its list archives with it; the messages survived at
[The Mail Archive](https://www.mail-archive.com/undertype-users@gna.org/maillist.html),
and are kept here in case that copy disappears too.

The whole list is also available as an
[mbox file](/history/undertype-users.mbox) for a mail client. Addresses are
hidden, as they were in The Mail Archive's copy.
