Hi The Peach,

Slowdowns : 
Depending of what version or even SVN revision of Fontmatrix you run, you 
might encouter performance issue. It’s mostly related to the fact that I try, 
at this beginning stage, to get more features and just have a look at 
performance when it becomes unusable - it happens more than I’d wanted :)
When features will be more stable, we’ll pay more attention to this field.

Font-book :  
You’ll want to reduce the length of the sample text to have more space for the 
third font.
The code that generates fontbook is really ugly and will be rewrote soon.
I see the font-book generation as one of the great feature of Fontmatrix and 
would like to make it the "definitive" way of producing an automatic 
font-book (yes, I’m kind of optimistic this evening). So, if you have 
requirements or ideas about what you want to see here, it will be listened 
very carefully.
