The Libre Graphics Meeting brings together developers and users of free
software graphics applications, such as the [GIMP](https://www.gimp.org/),
[Inkscape](https://inkscape.org/), [Scribus](https://www.scribus.net/),
[Blender](https://www.blender.org/), [Krita](https://krita.org/), the
[Open Clipart Library](https://openclipart.org/) and more.

In its third edition, the organization needs your help! You can support your
favorite graphics application, and ensure that the travel costs of as many
volunteer developers as possible are paid to ensure that this edition of the
conference is more successful that its predecessors.
